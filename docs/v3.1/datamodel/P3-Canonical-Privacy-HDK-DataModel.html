1 · Overview

Identity layers L2–L4
App Identity · Membership · Persona
App Identity (L2) per (person, app); Tenant Membership (L3) per (app_identity, tenant); Persona (L4) per typed kind. Extensions live in app pools.
G5 closer
person_pool_residency
First-touch registry pinpointing every pool a person's data lives in. DSAR fan-out across 200+ pools becomes verifiable.
G6 closer
hdk-sync + Conflict Resolution Model
Offline write queue + replay engine; five strategies (CRDT · LWW · merge · event-sourcing · human-review) declared per event type.
