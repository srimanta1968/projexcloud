1 · Overview

Domain SDKs
Field + Evidence
Evidence is the last domain SDK to ship — its chain-of-custody depends on every encryption tier, audit chain, and lineage edge being in place.
G10 closer
Pool Federation Runtime
Real regional federation router (not just hooks from P1). Activates when pool count exceeds 200. Per-region capacity; auto-failover for Tier-G.
G11 closer
Iceberg full federation
All cross-pool reports route through Iceberg, not Warehouse ClickHouse alone. Per-region Iceberg catalog.
Mode flip — meter hard caps. P1 = emit-only · P4 = soft caps (WARN headers) · P7 = hard caps (DENY). 30 weeks of meter data have calibrated the catalog by this point. Tenants past quota_policy.hard_cap receive QuotaExceeded responses; alert paths fire for ops + tenant admin.
