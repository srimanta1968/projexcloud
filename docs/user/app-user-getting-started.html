Application User — Getting Started

For end users of an application built on ProjexCloud — your sign-in, your persona, and how your data is protected.

Part of the onboarding suite. See onboarding-overview for the three-portal model and the identity model. Persona guides: Tenant Admin, Tenant Developer, App User, Platform Admin, ProjexCloud Admin.

Who you are

You are an everyday user of an application powered by ProjexCloud — a Buyer, Patient, Operator, Devotee, Contractor, or any role your organization defines. You do not manage the platform; you use the app.

Signing in

  1. Open the application URL your organization gave you and sign in (email/password, SSO, or a magic link invite).
  2. Your identity resolves to a persona — the role you hold in this app and tenant. You only see what your persona is allowed to see.
  3. If your organization uses MFA or biometric/PIN (on mobile via the HDK), complete that step.

Your data & privacy

  • Your personal data is encrypted and referenced by a canonical identity, never copied around as plain text.
  • Reads of personal data are consent-gated and audited — every access is logged in a tamper-evident ledger.
  • You can exercise data rights (access / export / erasure) through your organization’s admin, backed by sdk-data-rights.

Getting help

For access problems, role changes, or data requests, contact your organization’s Tenant Admin — they manage members, roles and consent for your tenant. Platform-level issues are handled by ProjexCloud operators behind the scenes.
