Tenant Admin — Onboarding Guide

Run your organization on ProjexCloud: people, money, integrations, and governance for one tenant.

Part of the onboarding suite. See onboarding-overview for the three-portal model and the identity model. Persona guides: Tenant Admin, Tenant Developer, App User, Platform Admin, ProjexCloud Admin.

Who you are & where you sign in

You administer one tenant — your organization inside an application. You sign in at /tenant (port 3200) with your ProjexCloud identity; your tenant membership carries the tenant-admin role. The same login also opens Workspace if your membership has a builder role.

First login. The first user of a new tenant is auto-promoted to tenant admin. Everyone else you add becomes a member with the persona/role you assign.

Day-1 checklist

  1. Confirm your billing plan and add a payment method.
  2. Invite your team by email; assign each person a persona/role; create Business Units if you have multiple sites/regions.
  3. Issue an API key for your first integration (store the secret once — it is shown only at creation).
  4. Set your AI provider keys (or enable BYOK) so AI features bill to your account, not the platform markup.
  5. Register a webhook endpoint for the events you care about and confirm signature verification.
  6. Review consent purposes and approval routes before going live with personal data.

Members, personas & roles — the model

A member = a person + a tenant membership + one or more personas. The persona is the assignable identity ("hat"); a role maps the persona to permissions; a Business Unit groups personas under their own approval chain. Personas can be human (invited by email) or service (machine identities for integrations and, in robotics, robots).

Troubleshooting

  • A member can’t sign in — check their invite was accepted and their membership status is active; revoked memberships invalidate sessions immediately.
  • API call returns 401 — the key was revoked or the JWT expired; re-issue or re-authenticate.
  • AI calls billed unexpectedly — set tenant AI provider keys / BYOK so token cost goes to your provider, not the platform governance SKU.
