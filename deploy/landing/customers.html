Use cases · every niche market

Pain → ProjexCloud → outcome, by vertical

Built for next-generation physical AI and regulated enterprise workflows alike. Each vertical starts from one workflow you can prove in weeks.

Physical AI · Humanoid & robotics OEMs

Fleet data & operations

Pain: TB–PB of multimodal sensor data, fragile fleet logging, custom portals per customer, and ungoverned AI-generated code.
ProjexCloud: fleet data backbone with multi-tenant isolation, analytics, AI Gateway, and usage metering per robot, site or customer.
Projexlight: governed delivery of fleet operations consoles, incident timelines and internal tools.
Faster robot iteration, safer releases, production-grade OEM portals.
Logistics · Warehouse robotics

Throughput & downtime analytics

Pain: multiple sites, fragmented telemetry, limited downtime root-cause visibility, ad-hoc dashboards.
ProjexCloud: ingest and store telemetry, incidents and SLA data per site and customer with precise metering.
Projexlight: operations dashboards, incident timelines, SLA views and alert workflows — governed end to end.
Quantifiable throughput gains and SLA compliance in one stack.
Industry 4.0 · Smart factory

Quality & traceability on sensor data

Pain: disconnected quality systems, underused sensor data, manual compliance/audit across lines and plants.
ProjexCloud: production events, process parameters and sensor readings with tenant isolation and tamper-evident audit.
Projexlight: quality portals, defect analytics and compliance reports tied to BDD tests and requirements traceability.
Audit-ready traceability and defect reduction you can trust.
Heavy manufacturing

Predictive maintenance from sensor streams

Pain: expensive unplanned downtime, raw sensor feeds underused, scattered maintenance workflows.
ProjexCloud: time-series sensor data, work orders and event logs in one tenant-aware platform per plant or asset group.
Projexlight: maintenance dashboards, failure-prediction insights and scheduling workflows, built and validated on that data.
Measurable reduction in downtime and maintenance cost.
